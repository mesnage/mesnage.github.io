Research Areas
Fasting & Longevity

The science of therapeutic fasting

As Scientific Director at Buchinger Wilhelmi — the world's foremost centre for therapeutic fasting — I design clinical studies, analyse patient data from thousands of fasting episodes, and translate findings into evidence-based protocols.

Our research investigates how prolonged fasting reshapes the gut microbiome, resets metabolic function, and drives measurable improvements in inflammatory markers, cardiovascular health, and quality of life.

Pesticide Toxicology

Understanding the true cost of agricultural chemicals

Over 80 publications and 6,500+ citations on glyphosate safety, co-formulant hazards, gut microbiome disruption, and the carcinogenic potential of herbicide formulations — work that has directly informed EU regulatory reform.

My research identified that surfactant-based co-formulants are up to 1,000 times more toxic than their active ingredients, contributing to the banning of certain ethoxylated tallowamines from glyphosate products in Europe.

Pesticide research
Plasticizers & EDCs

Exposing the risks hidden in everyday plastics

Bisphenol A and its commercial replacements disrupt the body's hormone signalling and stimulate breast cancer cell growth. Our work — supported by a £100,000 Breast Cancer UK grant — showed that many "BPA-free" alternatives are equally or more estrogenic than BPA.

The study was highlighted on the cover of Toxicological Sciences and cited by the US EPA and New York State legislation, showing that chemical replacements must be rigorously tested before reaching commerce.

Plasticizers research
GM Crops

Multi-omics evaluation of herbicide-tolerant crops

Beginning with the first long-term toxicity study on Roundup-tolerant GM maize in 2012, my work helped establish multi-omics approaches as the gold standard for detecting molecular changes invisible to conventional regulatory tests.

The Séralini affair became a defining case of corporate interference in independent science, raising fundamental questions about how safety evidence for commercialised crops is produced and reviewed.
