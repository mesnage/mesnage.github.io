September 2026 visit planner — 4 days, whole family

La rentrée is Tuesday 1 September 2026, so from the first week of September every school is in normal session — the best possible time to see them for real. Suggested window: Wednesday 16 – Saturday 19 September (or 23–26): shoulder-season prices, warm sea, no summer traffic, and the days line up with the local market calendar below. Book the two school tours 3–4 weeks ahead.

Day 1 · Wed — Roquefort-les-Pins & next-door villages

  • Morning: Roquefort-les-Pins Wednesday market (halles, 8–12) — feel the village with the kids; walk the Plans/Notre-Dame centre and the clinic surroundings.
  • Drive-bys: village primaries (Jean Camp / Roger Baud), Collège César, École Maria Mater — all within 5 min.
  • Afternoon: loop Le Rouret → Opio → Châteauneuf (Pré-du-Lac) on the D2085/D4 — the three quiet-village alternatives; stop at a Le Rouret agency (Nestenn / Opio Rouret Immobilier).
  • Evening: dinner in Roquefort-les-Pins — test the "living here" feel.

Day 2 · Thu — Schools day in Sophia / Valbonne

  • 08:00: drive Roquefort → Sophia via D3 — the real school-run commute test (~15 min off-peak, 25–35 at peak).
  • Morning: booked individual tour at ICS Côte d'Azur (60–90 min); drive past CIV campus and Les Colibris Montessori (5 min apart). Optional: EIB Cagnes receives visitors Tue/Thu mornings only — alternative slot.
  • Lunch: Valbonne old village square; walk the arcaded streets.
  • Afternoon: Parc de la Valmasque playgrounds + Fontmerle pond for the kids; estate agents in Valbonne (Blue Square / John Taylor).

Day 3 · Fri — Valbonne market, Mougins & the west

  • Morning: Valbonne Friday market (8–13) — the family-life benchmark; then booked visit at Mougins British International School (late morning).
  • Lunch: Mougins old village (Picasso's village — kid-tolerant, short walk).
  • Afternoon: Verrerie de Biot glassblowers (free to watch, great with a 3- and 5-year-old, 10–13/14–18) or the Mougins bike park; drive-by Mouans-Sartoux (organic-canteen town) and Les Pouces Verts on the way back.

Day 4 · Sat — The east: La Colle, St-Paul, Vence + sea

  • Morning: La Colle-sur-Loup Saturday market + village (best-value option); short hop to Saint-Paul-de-Vence ramparts walk.
  • Midday: Vence — market square lunch, drive past École Freinet and La Sine.
  • Afternoon: beach time at Cros-de-Cagnes or Parc de Vaugrenier playground (Villeneuve-Loubet); note Marineland is permanently closed (since Jan 2025) — don't plan around it.
  • Evening: family debrief — score the villages against your criteria (commute, schools, nature, budget).

How enrolment works

  • Public primary: enrol at the mairie of your commune (proof of address, livret de famille, vaccinations); school assigned by catchment (carte scolaire).
  • Public collège: automatic sectorisation by home address — Roquefort-les-Pins → Collège César; Le Rouret/Opio/Châteauneuf → Pré des Roures; La Colle/St-Paul → Yves Klein; Biot → l'Éganaude.
  • International sections (ASEICA English / APEG German): academy-run language tests override the catchment — register ~Dec–Jan, tests in March, results late March. CIV collège admits only via these tests.
  • Private schools (sous contrat & international): apply directly, any commune — no catchment. Popular year groups at ICS & Mougins BIS carry waitlists; apply ~18 months ahead.

Costs at a glance

  • Public: free from age 3; cantine ≈ €3–6/meal (often income-indexed), périscolaire a few €/session.
  • Language-section associations: ASEICA English — primary €2,846–3,683/yr, collège €1,900–2,459/yr; APEG German at CIV ≈ €1,350/yr.
  • Sous contrat: roughly €340–2,240/yr, usually income-banded (published: Stanislas, Ste-Marie de Chavagnes) + demi-pension.
  • International private: €9,400–25,600/yr + registration €1,800–3,500 + application ≈ €690; Montessori €9,300–11,200/yr.

Getting there & school transport

  • D2085 is the spine — everything in Roquefort-les-Pins hangs off it; pinch points at Pré-du-Lac and the Cagnes descent to the A8 (exit 47, 11 min).
  • School buses: Envibus (CASA) line 26 to Valbonne/Sophia + dedicated scolaire lines (4S, 5S, 17S, 20S…) and named primary circuits inside the village; enrol via the mairie's Portail Famille.
  • Sophia school run: 10–11 km, ~15 min off-peak via D3; 25–35 min at the 08:00 inflow.
  • Rail: no station on the plateau — coastal TER Cannes↔Nice (~4/h; TGV at Antibes; park-and-ride at Cagnes-sur-Mer, 17 min) + Cannes↔Grasse branch (~2/h; Mouans-Sartoux 20 min for the west). ZOU 650 bus Nice↔Grasse runs along the D2085 through the village; airport 25 min.

Your pathway (from your Schools Guide)

  • Phase 1 — arrival 2028–29: both children at ICS Côte d'Azur (bilingual FR/EN, IB PYP, ~13 min) — son into PY2–PY3, daughter into KG2/PY1.
  • Phase 2 — from age 11: transition to the CIV German section (APEG, free, ~15 min) → French Bac (OIB) + German qualification.
  • Nearby fallbacks: public sector schools in Roquefort-les-Pins itself (village primaries + Collège César, 96% brevet, English int'l section) cost €0 and rank among the best in the département.
  • Before the move: ICS waitlist registration; APEG/CIV open day; note the March SI test window for collège entry.

Method & caveats

Compiled 26 July 2026. Brevet (DNB) rates, mentions and IPS: DEPP session 2025 via lucyol.fr / enseigna.fr / Onisep. Fees: school websites and international-schools-database.com (2025–26 or 2026–27 schedules as noted); sous contrat estimates in italics where schools don't publish. Map base: coastline & commune boundaries from official Etalab/IGN administrative data (france-geojson); principal roads hand-traced from the real network (indicative — live OSM/IGN road services are not reachable from this workspace); school coordinates approximate; driving times are typical off-peak estimates — verify live before committing. School-day and after-school-care times: school/mairie websites, July 2026 — many schools publish these only to enrolled parents, so entries marked "typ." are the standard pattern for that school type, to confirm directly. Choropleth: MeilleursAgents average house €/m² per commune, May–Jul 2026 (estimates, not notarial data). Railway alignment traced from the real network (indicative); station positions approximate. Google-review scores were not used (not reliably retrievable). Ratings for private schools rest on accreditation and inspection status, as France publishes no league tables for primary or private-hors-contrat schools.
