How kids' activities work in France

  • The season runs September–June. Sign-ups happen in the first two weeks of September — every village holds a Forum des Associations (usually the first or second Saturday) where all clubs have stands and take registrations. If you visit in mid-September, most clubs still take late entries and offer a free trial session.
  • Paperwork: a licence + simple health attestation (questionnaire for minors; a médecin's certificate only in some sports), one photo, and the fee.
  • Typical cost: €150–400/year for a weekly club activity — a fraction of German private-lesson pricing. Municipal écoles des sports (e.g. Cagnes "Visa Sports") are even cheaper.
  • Wednesday is the activities day — no school for primaires, so clubs run children's sessions Wednesday plus Saturday morning.

Shortlist for your two (ages ~5 & 8)

  • In the village (0–5 min): AS Roquefort football school (from U6), the municipal dojo's judo club, the skatepark + city-stade, and the École de Musique Associative (éveil musical from ~4) — all at or near the Complexe sportif / Centre culturel on the D2085.
  • 10–15 min: Nautipolis swim school (jardin aquatique from 4), COV Valbonne (gym, judo and a dozen other sections), Valbonne conservatoire, Bouillides skatepark.
  • Weekend nature: Valmasque forest + Fontmerle pond (lotus bloom in August), Vaugrenier's big playgrounds, Brague river shade in summer, Gorges du Loup waterfalls.
  • From ~age 7: Optimist sailing at Cagnes' municipal école de voile (Jardin des Mers from 5 in summer) or YCVL at Marina Baie des Anges.

Water & beach notes

  • Family beaches: Plage du Ponteil (Antibes) is the classic shallow, sheltered kids' beach; Cros-de-Cagnes is the nearest sandy-gravel beach with lifeguards in season.
  • Flags: green/yellow/red lifeguard system mid-June–mid-Sept; most beaches unguarded off-season.
  • Pools: Nautipolis is the family flagship (lagoon, slides, lessons); municipal pools in Antibes, Cagnes, Vence and Grasse are cheaper for lengths and school-holiday swim courses.
  • Note: Marineland (Antibes) closed permanently in Jan 2025 — the nearby kids' brands (Kid's Island/Adventure Golf) partially wound down too; check status before promising a visit.
